Zero-Pilferage Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

3-Way Zero Pilferage Golden Rules

1. Gate Pass vs Loaded Qty Balance: No vehicle can leave factory/depot without Security Gate Pass matching the exact sum of invoices.

2. Evening Physical Stock Return Audit: Any undelivered/returned product MUST physically enter the depot cold room / store within 1 hour of vehicle return.

3. Cash & Invoice Reconciliation before Driver Release: Delivery Man/Driver cannot leave depot premises before Cash handover to Cashier.
