⚡ Poloxy ERP Auto Sync (Direct 1-Click Pull) Automated
Direct ERP Login & Database Auto-Save

Automatically logs in to Poloxy ERP (erp.paragon.com.bd), extracts Orders, Delivered (Today & MTD), Closing Stock across depots for Frozen Foods, Process Chicken, and Branded Eggs, calculates daily sales averages and stock covers, and saves directly into the database.

💡 Tip: For a specific single date report, keep both From Date and To Date identical. It will extract only that date's exact data (no unwanted MTD). Or set different dates to extract that exact date range.
